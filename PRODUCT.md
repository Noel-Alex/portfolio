## Platform

## Users

- Recruiters, hiring managers and research leads deciding in a few minutes whether Noel is worth a conversation. They want the strongest builds, real numbers, education, availability and a way to make contact.
- Engineers, GDG VIT peers and Minecraft players who arrive curious and will explore if the site rewards it.

## Product Purpose

Noel Alex's personal portfolio. It shows what Noel builds (systems, research tooling, applied ML), how he works (measures, labels limits honestly) and who he is with other people (GDG VIT, teaching, friends). Success: visitors stay, explore, remember it, and reach out.

## Positioning

The portfolio is itself a modded-Minecraft world, because Noel plays and loves modded Minecraft (Create, Twilight Forest, Applied Energistics) and builds the same way: big contraptions, benchmarked past the point of reason, shared with friends.

## Operating Context

Static site on GitHub Pages at `/portfolio/` (Vite + React + TypeScript, deployed by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). Visited on desktop and phones, often from a LinkedIn or GitHub link.
