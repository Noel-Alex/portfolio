Scope: the whole portfolio (one SPA). Visitor mode: Experience, with a recruiter fast path.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a modded Minecraft client you boot: title screen, mod list, server list, world and guidebooks are the site's real navigation. It refuses a dark landing page with pixel headings over project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Minecraft GUI material on a live voxel world: bevelled stone buttons, #C6C6C6 inventory panels with white/grey bevels, black-purple tooltips, yellow splash text, leather guidebook with cream pages. Monocraft type. World colours: grass, oak, Create brass and andesite, AE2 fluix purple, Twilight firefly green, night navy.

STORY: Visitors see Noel builds big systems and measures them honestly, play a little, find projects and people, and email or open the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed real-time 3D floating island panorama rotating slowly, softened. Voxel stone "NOEL ALEX" logo upper centre with a tilted pulsing splash. Centre button stack: Singleplayer (primary), Multiplayer, Mods, then Options and Resume half-width. Bottom-left version line with degree, school and year; bottom-right fan-made notice.

FORM: Minecraft client UI (pinned, list position 1). seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: Singleplayer swoops the camera onto the player at spawn; clicking a build walks the player there and opens its guidebook.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
